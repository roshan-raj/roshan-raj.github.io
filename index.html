A lot of my work sits where messy data meets clean architecture. I bring complex relational data into graph databases like Neo4j, design high-throughput pipelines, and modernise infrastructure with AWS CDK.

I also act as the bridge between product and engineering. I turn ambiguous business goals into clear technical requirements, phased roadmaps and architecture that teams can ship with confidence.

  1. Mar 2023 — Present

    Technical Engineer

    Koch Inc. · Bengaluru

    Driving backend and cloud architecture for a core enterprise analytics and reporting platform. I partner with product managers and business analysts to turn complex reporting needs into fast, scalable data services.

    • Enterprise event bus. Architected a decoupled communication layer on AWS EventBridge and SQS, with idempotency and dead-letter patterns across 50+ integrations to keep transactions intact across distributed systems.
    • Graph-based data strategy. Helped move siloed Oracle/SQL schemas to a Neo4j graph model, and optimised ingestion for dense relationships to power C3.ai predictive engines.
    • SST to AWS CDK migration. Leading the move of the whole service landscape to AWS CDK for finer resource control, stronger security compliance and a standard deployment lifecycle.
    • Reusable CDK constructs. Co-designed shared constructs and templates that standardise architecture across the organisation and make every new service secure by design.
    • Post-deploy validation. Built a custom Effect-based testing framework that audits permissions after each deployment, drastically cutting production access-denied incidents.
    • Modernisation. Led a multi-service upgrade to Node.js v24 with TypeScript and TDD, keeping 100% service uptime throughout.
    • Product ↔ engineering bridge. Turned ambiguous business goals into technical requirements, phased roadmaps and architectural blueprints, including the C3.ai enterprise AI integration.
